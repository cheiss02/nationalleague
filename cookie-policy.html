This site does not use cookies

National League Official Academies does not set any cookies, and does not use Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, or any other tracking technology. Fonts are self-hosted and registration forms are linked (not embedded), so no third-party cookies are set by visiting this site either.

What we do store

The site stores one thing in your browser's localStorage (not a cookie): your language preference, and whether you've dismissed the "¿Prefieres español?" hint. This is strictly necessary/functional, stays on your device, and is never sent to us or anyone else.

Why there is no cookie banner

A cookie banner is required when a site sets non-essential cookies or uses tracking technology. This site does neither, so no banner is shown. If that ever changes — for example, if we add privacy-friendly analytics or any advertising technology — this page and the site will be updated first, and any non-essential tracking will sit behind a real consent banner with equally visible Accept/Reject options.

Changes

If our use of cookies or similar technology changes, this policy will be updated to match before the change takes effect.
